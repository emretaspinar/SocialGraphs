## Query: Inception

Dom Cobb and Arthur are professional thieves who perform corporate espionage using experimental dream-sharing technology to infiltrate their targets' subconscious and extract information. Their latest target Saito is impressed with Cobb's ability to layer multiple dreams within each other. He offers to hire Cobb for the ostensibly impossible job of performing "inception": implanting Robert Fischer, the son of Saito's competitor Maurice Fischer, with the idea to dissolve his father's conglomerate. In return, Saito promises to clear Cobb's criminal status, allowing him to return home to his children. Cobb accepts the offer and recruits a forger named Eames, a chemist named Yusuf, and an architecture student named Ariadne. They devise a three-level dream structure in order for Robert to accept the implanted idea as his own.
